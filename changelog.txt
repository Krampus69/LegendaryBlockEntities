legendaryblockentities-1.20.1 Changelog
0.9.0
====
Vanilla chests, beds, bells and shulker boxes no longer turn invisible when another mod
shuffles resource pack priority (seen with Tectonic)
Their models are now bound in code instead of relying on LBE blockstate files winning
Rotated models no longer cull the wrong face against neighbouring blocks, most visible
on attached shulker boxes
